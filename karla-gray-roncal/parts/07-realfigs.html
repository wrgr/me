Real figures — what the licences actually allow

Three figures we are allowed to show you.

Of the nine indexed items in this bibliography, exactly one carries a licence permitting reproduction. These are its figures, unmodified except for scaling, with full attribution. Everything else on this page that looks like a chart, we drew.

What we could not show, and why

Neurology 2021. Free to read in PubMed Central, and still © American Academy of Neurology, all rights reserved — there is no Creative Commons licence on it. Its single figure, a mirror-image plot of grey matter below the line and lesion volume above it, is the best graphic in her whole record. Plate XIII is our redrawing from the published table values, which are facts and not copyrightable. It is not that figure.

Multiple Sclerosis Journal 2026. Free to read at the publisher with no open licence — “bronze” access, which confers no reuse rights at all. Nothing from it is reproduced here.

The IEEE conference papers are closed access. The medRxiv preprint is CC BY-NC-ND, whose no-derivatives term we read as excluding the rescaling this page would require, so we left it alone. The two submarine papers come from a classified symposium and have no public existence to reproduce.

Original unmodified extractions are kept in figs/ alongside a provenance note, so anyone can check what was changed.
