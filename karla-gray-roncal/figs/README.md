No cropping, relabelling or recolouring was applied to any of them. They were extracted
from the publisher PDF at `https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-08078-3.pdf`, which
is not committed here (1.5 MB, and freely re-downloadable).

**Extraction note worth keeping:** the two thin images on page 7 of the PDF appear in the
image table in the opposite order to their captions. Verified against the caption
y-positions on the page: xref 141 (y 50–98) sits above the *Figure 3* caption, xref 140
(y 151–191) above the *Figure 4* caption. The filenames here reflect the corrected
mapping. Getting this backwards inverts the paper's easy/hard argument.
